?Quick How to Play · Revision 3.3

Goal

Business: catch the historical Raider. Bandit side: keep that actual thief out of the final valid catch set. Current Card movement never transfers Raider history.
1 · DEALBackend randomly deals one immutable Starting Role per occupied seat plus 3 hidden Hammock cards. Current Cards can move later.
2 · DICE/1/ = d6 · /2/ = d12 · deliberate joystick flick = roll. Raw results are private. Duplicate multi-wake d12s reroll only the duplicate.
3 · HOUR ORDERWake → Treat State before action → Raider theft → conversion/witness triggers → Starting-Role action → commit → sleep.
4 · EVIDENCEFacts can be timestamped and later become stale. Public claims are never auto-verified; inference is not a fact.
5 · DISCUSSChat stays open through voting. Prefer HOUR → TREAT STATE → CO-WAKERS → ROLE FACT → DEDUCTION. Quick Role Claim can build that testimony, but it is never proof. Starting Role and Current Card claims stay separate; only Starting Role determines scheduled actions.
6 · PAW POINTGuardian protection is separate from Guardian’s own vote and removes one other player from capture eligibility first. Then one private vote each locks simultaneously; all eligible tied leaders are caught.
7 · RESOLVEPaw Point catches resolve first → a valid loyal Hunter Hunt Mark may add a catch → Snuggle Bond may reveal a caught player’s partner → then the historical Raider win check resolves. Bond-Revealed is not caught.

Narrator

The Narrator panel uses your current player session automatically. Shared lines may be spoken; private role instructions stay local/headphone-gated. Narrator speech obeys the HUD sound control: 🔇 silences it, while 🔉/🔊 set its volume. The host narrator can also post shared cues into the public game channels as Ferret Frenzy Narrator; private facts never go to public chat.

Role gotchas

Dooker: Glimpse = current allegiance at that moment and may become stale. Business: a lone wake inspects one random wake result, not a whole schedule. Trouble: theft-window overlap converts before the swap and learns the Raider. Itchy: a valid two-name clue contains the historical Raider; a no-decoy edge case must be marked degraded. Snuggler: Bond-Reveal is a result reveal, not another Paw Point catch. Hunter: converted Hunter gets no Hunt Mark effect. Guardian: starting-Hunter recognition can become stale trust.

State separation

Starting Role sets scheduled actions. Current Card can move. Current Allegiance can change. Raider history, Trouble/Hunter conversion, bond, protection, and Hunt Mark stay attached to the player/event they belong to.

Bots

Bots are dealt randomly like humans. They reason from authorized facts plus public claims, and they do not expose a hidden role in chat unless they deliberately volunteer a claim or the role becomes public.
⌨Ferret Frenzy Controls

Dice

/1/ selects d6 action/chance. /2/ selects d12 wake-hour. Online rolls are server-authoritative and private.

Joystick

Normal movement navigates. A deliberate flick rolls the selected die.

Number buttons

/1/ selects d6. /2/ selects d12. Those are the only numbered gameplay buttons.

Action buttons

A confirm/select · B back/cancel · X context-sensitive role action. Unused Y/Z/R buttons were removed.
