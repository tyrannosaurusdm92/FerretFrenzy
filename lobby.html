FERRET FRENZY FIELD GUIDE

How to Play

Reconstruct one chaotic night, then Paw Point the historical Raider.

Revision 3.3 keeps the night short and private: twelve Burrow Hours generate evidence, Morning Business turns evidence into testimony, and one simultaneous vote resolves the social contest.

1 · Round setup

The active set uses the player-count recipe plus three hidden Hammock cards. The backend shuffles it and deals one private Starting Card to every occupied seat. Bots never reserve roles; they fill seats first and receive roles from the same random deal as humans.

Keep three ideas separate: Starting Role determines scheduled night actions and never changes. Current Card can move. Current Allegiance is derived from Current Card plus player-bound history such as Raider, Trouble conversion, or Hunter conversion.

2 · The eight roles

BanditSable / Black Sable

Starting Bandits know the starting Bandit roster. The earliest starting-Bandit wake is the historical Raider and must steal the Minnow Treats.

DookerSilver Mitt

Three unique wakes: observe; Glimpse one player’s current allegiance at that moment; then view one Hammock card and optionally Trip it onto one player. A Glimpse can become stale later.

ItchyCinnamon

d6 5–6 activates a second distinct wake. After the theft, receive a two-player clue containing the historical Raider plus one legal non-Raider decoy when possible. If no legal decoy exists, the clue must be clearly marked degraded rather than pretending one name is a full pair.

TroubleChampagne

Blind-swap the Current Cards of two distinct other players. If present in the theft window, become In On It before swapping and learn the historical Raider identity.

BusinessWarm Sable Roan

One ordinary wake. If genuinely alone at the start of that Burrow Hour, choose one other player and inspect exactly one randomly selected wake result, never their whole schedule.

SnugglerChocolate

Before Hour 1, bond two distinct other players. Partners learn each other’s identity, not role/allegiance. If one is caught, the partner may be Bond-Revealed in results; that reveal is not another Paw Point catch.

HunterBlaze

Recognizes Guardian and wakes twice. First overlap with a starting Bandit triggers one private d6; 1–2 permanently converts Hunter. A still-loyal Hunter later sets one conditional Hunt Mark.

GuardianDark-Eyed White

Recognizes the starting Hunter but never learns whether Hunter converts. Immediately before Paw Point locks, protect one other player by removing them from vote-capture eligibility.

3 · Dice and private setup

1 selects d6. 2 selects d12. A deliberate joystick flick commits the required roll. d12 values are private Burrow Hours; d6 is used only for Itchy activation, Hunter conversion, and tied earliest-Bandit Raider selection. Multi-wake roles reroll only duplicate d12 values until their own hours are unique.

4 · Same-hour order

Every Burrow Hour resolves in this exact order: wake scheduled players → show Treat State before action → Raider theft if this is the theft hour → witness/conversion triggers → Starting-Role actions → commit private facts → sleep again. This matters: Trouble converts before its blind swap, and a Dooker Glimpse in the theft hour sees allegiance after conversion triggers for that window.

5 · Sleep and the crime scene

Sleeping keeps the black Ferret Frenzy logo screen inside the game viewport while the shell/HUD and Burrow Clock remain visible. Non-identifying chittering can signal hostile-side activity without naming who is awake. When entitled to wake, you see your own Treat State, co-wakers, and legal prompt only.

The historical Raider’s theft window enables the Minnow Treat GeoJSON hotspot. The Raider taps it to commit the crime and transition their authorized scene from PRESENT to MISSING. Later authorized wakes see MISSING; ordinary MISSING is timing evidence, not an automatic Raider reveal.

6 · Evidence, claims, and stale facts

Hard personal facts are things the game directly tells you: your rolls, Treat State, co-wakers, legal scans/inspections, conversion result, bond/recognition, or your own target choices. Claims are what players say publicly and may be true, mistaken, selective, or false. Inferences are deductions built from those pieces. The app never turns confidence into proof or publishes a guilt score.

Timestamp facts. “Dooker Glimpsed Alex Bandit-aligned at Hour 4” is not the same as “Alex is Bandit-aligned at dawn.” Card movement or conversion can make a true earlier fact stale without making it a lie.

7 · Card movement and player-bound history

Trouble swaps two player Current Cards blindly. Dooker can place one known Hammock card onto a player without seeing the displaced card. These moves never rewrite Starting Role actions or move the historical Raider, Trouble In On It status, Hunter conversion, Snuggle Bond, Guardian protection, or Hunt Mark.

Allegiance precedence is: historical Raider is Bandit-side; converted Trouble/Hunter remain Bandit-side; otherwise a Bandit Current Card means Bandit-side and a non-Bandit Current Card means Business-side. Receiving a Bandit card does not retroactively reveal the starting Bandit roster or make that player the historical Raider.

8 · Morning Business and Quick Role Claim

After Hour 12, night actions freeze and shared chat stays open through voting. Public Discord-inspired channels organize the same shared conversation into #burrow, #evidence, #claims, #paw-point, and #narrator; they do not create hidden faction rooms. The host can optionally enable Turn Mode: players Raise Paw, the host grants the next turn, and only the current floor-holder can type until they yield. Discuss hours, Treat State, co-wakers, card movement, bonds, recognition, possible conversions, and role claims. The Quick Role Claim dropdown is only a statement builder: it does not compare your claim to hidden server truth, so truthful claims and bluffs use the same UI. Choose whether the claim describes your Starting Role, Current Card, or both being the same; those are not interchangeable because only Starting Role determines scheduled actions. You can optionally add claimed wake hour(s), Treat State, a co-waker, and role-specific details so testimony follows the useful order HOUR → TREAT STATE → CO-WAKERS → ROLE FACT → DEDUCTION.

Bots follow the same rule. A bot may volunteer a public role claim, but direct questions cannot force its hidden Starting Role out of private state. Until a voluntary claim or public reveal, bot chat must stay within role-neutral evidence it is authorized to discuss.

9 · Guardian protection and Paw Point

Immediately before Paw Point locks, Guardian may privately protect one other player. Protection and Guardian’s own Paw Point are separate choices; Guardian should not automatically protect Hunter just because the starting-role recognition was genuine. Votes cast at that protected player are still recorded for replay, but the protected player is removed from vote-capture eligibility. Every active player then locks exactly one private Paw Point simultaneously. Find the highest remaining eligible vote total and catch every eligible player tied at that total.

10 · Secondary resolution and winning

After the Paw Point catch set is known, resolve a valid loyal Hunter’s conditional Hunt Mark first, then resolve Snuggle Bond result reveals. A Hunt Mark catch can therefore trigger a later Bond-Reveal. Bond-Reveal exposes a bonded partner in results but is not itself a Paw Point or Hunt Mark capture. The primary question remains historical: did the final valid catch logic catch the player who actually stole the Minnow Treats? Business wins only if yes; Bandit side wins if the Raider escapes. Catching only an accomplice or converted Hunter is not enough.

Integrated Narrator

The game HUD includes a Narrator panel that uses the already-connected player seat. Shared phase announcements can be spoken aloud and the narrator speech volume follows the same HUD sound control as game audio: mute cancels/suppresses speech, while low/full sound set narrator volume. Private role instructions and private facts stay on that player’s device and are spoken only after headphones are confirmed. To prevent duplicate moderator messages, the host narrator owns public narrator chat: it can automatically post shared Morning Business, Paw Point, and Results cues, or send a manual public narrator line. Narrator chat never carries private prompts or private facts.

Solo + Bots

SOLO + BOTS creates ordinary guest bot seats, readies them, and lets the existing backend perform the same random active-set deal. Each bot loads the specialist brain for its own returned Starting Role only after the deal. Bot voting and chat are based on authorized facts plus public claims, never omniscient hidden state.

Privacy and replay

Raw d6/d12 results, hidden cards, secret conversions, Hammock views, and private action prompts stay on authorized views. Reconnection rebuilds only what that player is entitled to know. Post-game Trail of Trouble may explain hidden events after the round according to the available result/replay data, but replay never changes what was legal to know during play.
